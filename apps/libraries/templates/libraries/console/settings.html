How big a new cartridge is

A cartridge costs no disk until something writes to it - the files grow - so the size is about how long it takes to fill one, not how much room it takes. A real LTO-8 holds 12 TB, which is about 63 hours of writing: too long to reach the end of a tape, which is usually the thing worth testing. So a new cartridge is 1 GB unless you say otherwise here.
