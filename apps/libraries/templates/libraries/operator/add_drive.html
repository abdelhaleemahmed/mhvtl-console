How the drive is placed

  • The models offered are the ones this library's model takes, plus whatever it already holds.
  • The slot is the first free one, and the drive id and SCSI target are the next free of each - no two devices may share an address.
  • The serial is the library's own with the slot after it, unless you give one.
  • The library's daemons are restarted, so the drive appears at once.
